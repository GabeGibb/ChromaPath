Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 7, 2026

Summary: ChromaPath is a puzzle game that respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Introduction

Welcome to ChromaPath! This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

The app:

  • Does not require an account or registration
  • Does not collect your name, email, or any contact information
  • Does not track your location
  • Does not access your camera, microphone, or photos
  • Does not use analytics or tracking services
  • Does not contain advertisements

Network Requests

ChromaPath may make GET requests to our servers to generate puzzle boards. These requests:

  • Do not include any personal information
  • Do not include any device identifiers
  • Are used solely to generate game puzzles
  • Do not track or profile users in any way

No data from these requests is stored or logged on our servers.

Data Storage

Any game progress or settings are stored locally on your device only. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any servers
  • Is not accessible to us or any third parties
  • Can be deleted by uninstalling the app

Third-Party Services

ChromaPath does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data. We do not use:

  • Analytics platforms
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media integrations
  • Cloud storage services

Children's Privacy

ChromaPath is safe for users of all ages. Since we do not collect any personal information, there are no special concerns regarding children's privacy. The app complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) by design.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal information to access, modify, or delete. If you have any questions about your data, please contact us.
